About Us

Our mission is not just to run an organization, but to make selfless service (sewa) a way of life. We are deeply devoted to serving the sacred land of Braj, where every action is driven by faith, devotion, and compassion. Through our sewa activities, we aim to spread kindness, spiritual awareness, and support for every being in need. We believe that true happiness comes from serving others with love, humility, and dedication.

Braj Dham Sewa

We are committed to preserving the purity and spiritual essence of Braj Dham. Our efforts focus on protecting its divine environment and maintaining cleanliness in holy places. We actively participate in awareness drives and support initiatives that help preserve Braj’s sacred culture and traditions. Our goal is to keep Braj spiritually vibrant and peaceful for future generations.

Vrindavan Flood Sewa (2023, 2024, 2025)

During the floods in Vrindavan, we actively served affected families with dedication and compassion. We provided food, clothing, shelter, medicines, and financial support to people facing hardships. Our volunteers worked day and night to help those in urgent need during difficult situations. This sewa was done with the spirit of humanity and devotion.

Brajwasi Sewa

Serving the people of Braj is equal to serving the Divine. We continuously support Brajwasis with food, clothing, daily essentials, and financial help whenever needed. Our aim is to stand beside every needy family with love and care. We believe that every act of kindness strengthens humanity and devotion.

Sant & Animal Sewa

We offer dedicated service to saints with deep respect and devotion. Along with this, we care for animals by providing food, shelter, and protection. Our mission is driven by compassion towards every living being. Through this sewa, we aim to spread mercy, peace, and spiritual values in society.
